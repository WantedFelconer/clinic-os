You are an award-winning Senior Product Designer at Google, Stripe, and Linear with 15+ years of SaaS dashboard design experience.

Your task is to design the COMPLETE UI/UX of a modern SaaS web application called "ClinicOS".

The product is NOT a hospital management system.

ClinicOS is a SaaS platform where independent doctors and personal clinic owners create and manage their own digital clinics.

The design should be production-ready and comparable to Linear, Notion, Stripe Dashboard, Vercel Dashboard, and modern healthcare startups.

The design language should be:

• Minimal
• Premium
• Clean
• Highly organized
• Modern SaaS
• Rounded corners (10-16px)
• Soft shadows
• Excellent whitespace
• Beautiful typography
• Consistent spacing using an 8-point grid
• Professional healthcare color palette
